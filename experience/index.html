Career Background

The career began as an agronomist at Doktar Technologies, an agri-tech start-up, as one of its first employees. Built the customer database from day one, and grew the social media audience by 50,000 in a single year, which led to a paid service that became the company’s main source of income. As Digital Marketing Manager, defined requirements for the company’s custom CRM and CMS platform, ran user acceptance testing (UAT), built Power BI dashboards for senior teams and worked with major clients, including Bayer Crop Science, on website migrations in Adobe Experience Manager. At Puratos UK, promoted from QA Technician to QA Team Leader within six months, responsible for data quality and traceability, supported BRCGS AA/AA+ accreditation, and introduced and managed new business tools. Curious, forward-thinking and drawn to new challenges, the next step was Salesforce: certification and a complete practice org, built and tested from scratch.
