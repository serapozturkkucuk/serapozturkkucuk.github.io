Career background

Her responsibilities later grew to include digital marketing, CRM and product data, app testing, dashboards and website migrations. Process improvements delivered £80,000 in annual cost reductions. Salesforce administration is the next step, applying experience in data, systems, UAT and process improvement to configuration, automation and testing.
