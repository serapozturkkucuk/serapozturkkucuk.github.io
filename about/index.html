Experience

About me

The career started as an agronomist and copywriter at Doktar Technologies, an agri-tech start-up in Turkey, as one of its first employees. Built the customer database from day one, then grew the company’s social media audience by 50,000 in a year, which turned into a paid service and became the company’s main source of income. Went on to lead digital marketing, help develop the CRM and product database, and build dashboards for senior teams. After moving to the UK, joined Puratos in quality assurance, was promoted to QA Team Leader within a year, and delivered £80,000 in annual savings through process improvements. When the company relocated in 2026, that change became the starting point for the next step: Salesforce, where data, systems and process skills come together.
