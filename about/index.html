About me

{{ site.author.summary }}

I spent twelve years at Doktar Technologies, an agri-tech start-up in Turkey, where I was one of the first employees. I built the company's customer database from day one, helped develop its CRM and product database, ran digital marketing, and built dashboards for senior teams.

In the UK I moved into quality assurance at Puratos, a food manufacturer, and was promoted to QA Team Leader. That work was about accurate data, clear procedures and testing before anything reaches people.

Salesforce brings these together. I passed the Salesforce Certified Platform Administrator exam in 2026 and built a complete practice org to show what I can do.
