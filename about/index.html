About me

I started as an agronomist and copywriter at Doktar Technologies, an agri-tech start-up in Turkey, where I was one of the first employees. I built the customer database from day one and grew its social media audience by 50,000 in one year, helping establish a paid service that became the company’s main source of income. I later led digital marketing, helped develop the CRM and product database, and created dashboards for senior teams. After moving to the UK, I joined Puratos in quality assurance, was promoted to QA Team Leader within a year, and delivered £80,000 in annual savings through process improvements. When the company relocated in 2026, I chose Salesforce as my next step, bringing together my experience in data, systems and processes.
